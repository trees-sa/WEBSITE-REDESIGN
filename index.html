The IEEE Student Branch is where engineering students build things, break things, and figure out how they actually work — through workshops, competitions, and a direct line to the wider IEEE community.

2010Branch founded
4Core activity tracks
5M+Papers via IEEE Xplore

What this branch does

01 / ABOUT

Since 2010, the branch has run a steady mix of technical and professional programming — the kind of hands-on exposure that's hard to get from a syllabus alone. It's also been repeatedly recognised for how consistently students actually show up and engage.

Activities & initiatives

02 / PROGRAMS
N.01

Technical Workshops

Hands-on sessions on emerging tools and languages, run by faculty and industry practitioners.

N.02

Project Competitions

Annual hardware and software contests that push theory into working prototypes.

N.03

Industry Connect

Talks, site visits, and networking sessions with practising engineers.

N.04

Paper Presentations

Support and mentorship for students presenting research at IEEE conferences.

Why join

03 / BENEFITS
  • Full access to the IEEE Xplore library — 5M+ technical papers
  • A network of peers and professionals across the world
  • Reduced registration rates for IEEE conferences
  • Eligibility for IEEE scholarships, awards, and competitions
  • Real leadership experience through branch volunteering
